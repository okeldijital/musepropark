# Product & Editorial Mandate

MuseProPark is a media-first editorial platform for music producers and creative culture.

## What MuseProPark Is
- a curated knowledge ecosystem
- an editorial authority
- a long-term cultural archive
- a learning and thinking companion

## What MuseProPark Is Not
- a content farm
- a growth-hacked blog
- a forum-first community
- a marketplace disguised as editorial content

## Editorial Priorities
1. Trust over reach
2. Depth over frequency
3. Accuracy over speed
4. Experience over speculation

## Non-Negotiables
- No paid editorial influence
- No undisclosed affiliate incentives
- No SEO padding
- No algorithm-driven content decisions

Editorial integrity supersedes monetisation and growth.
